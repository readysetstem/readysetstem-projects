How speakers work

Speakers make sounds. They exist all around us in or lives - in phones, TVs, greeting cards, toys, musical keyboards, and video games. They allow us to directly play amplified sounds through electric megaphones, PA (public announcement) systems in schools and buildings, and loudspeakers at concerts. They can also play recorded sounds, or even create new made up sounds that you'd never hear in nature.

Speakers work by converting electrical energy into mechanical energy. That is, changes in the the voltage/current in a circuit can cause something physical to move back and forth.
